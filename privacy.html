Privacy Policy

Bourse Update for Everybody
Last updated: August 17, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Bourse Update for Everybody handles information when generating videos and connecting to TikTok through TikTok's official APIs.

2. Information We Process

The tool may process video files, captions, hashtags, generated content metadata, market data, and TikTok OAuth authorization tokens when a user chooses to connect a TikTok account.

3. TikTok Data

When you authorize TikTok access, the tool may receive an access token, refresh token, open ID, granted scopes, and basic account information allowed by the scopes you approved. These credentials are used only to upload or publish content on your behalf.

4. Storage

In the local development version, TikTok tokens are stored locally on the user's machine. They are not intentionally shared, sold, or transferred to third parties by this project.

5. Use of Information

Information is used to generate videos, prepare captions and hashtags, upload content to TikTok, check publishing status, and maintain the authorized TikTok connection.

6. Third-Party Services

The tool may use third-party services such as TikTok APIs and public market data providers. Those services are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Data Deletion

Users can disconnect TikTok access by revoking the app permission from their TikTok account settings and deleting the locally stored token files from their machine.

8. Security

Users should keep client secrets, access tokens, and refresh tokens private. Do not publish secrets or token files in public repositories.

9. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project owner through the GitHub repository where this page is hosted.
